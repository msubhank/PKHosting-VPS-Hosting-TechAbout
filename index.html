Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about billing, discounts, infrastructure, and technical management.

How does the 10-months-for-12 annual discount work?

When you choose annual billing, you only pay for 10 months up-front, and you receive a full 12-month service term. This translates to an effective 16.67% monthly discount (2 free months). For example, our Growth plan is Rs 4,800/month on monthly billing, but billed annually at Rs 48,000, reducing your effective per-month rate to only Rs 4,000/month.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my VPS resources later?

Yes. You can seamlessly scale your CPU cores, RAM, and NVMe disk space directly from the PKHosting client area at any time. Upgrades take effect with a quick reboot (under 60 seconds), and the billing difference is automatically calculated on a pro-rata basis.

What payment methods do you accept in Pakistan and globally?

For Pakistani clients paying in PKR, we accept instant mobile payments via JazzCash and EasyPaisa, online Direct Bank Transfer / 1Link IBFT with automated receipt matching, and all major domestic Visa & Mastercard debit/credit cards. International clients can pay seamlessly via Visa, Mastercard, or Bank Transfer in USD or GBP.

Do I get full root access and operating system freedom?

Every VPS comes with unrestricted root access over SSH. You can choose from standard enterprise Linux distributions (Ubuntu 22.04/24.04, Debian 12, AlmaLinux 9, Rocky Linux) or Windows Server on our Scale and Dedicated plans. You can also upload custom ISO images directly through the control panel.

Where are the data centers located, and what is the latency inside Pakistan?

Our Pakistani nodes are housed in Tier-3 data centers in Karachi and Islamabad connected to the Pakistan Internet Exchange (PIE) and local ISPs (PTCL, Nayatel, StormFiber, Transworld), delivering sub-15ms round-trip latency nationwide. Global nodes in Frankfurt, Singapore, and London are also available for international redundancy.
